## Suggested opening narration

“This is Neverlost: The Full Human Pathway. The problem is not only whether AI can summarize complex records. It is whether the summary keeps goals, observations, professional authority, recovery cost, and pending decisions separate.”

## Suggested demonstration narration

“This fictional draft says Maya is medically cleared, can sustain 20 hours, will receive equipment and training funding, and will keep her benefits. None of those conclusions are supported. The demo rebuilds the case from seven source records, classifies each authority, creates a capacity record and six-lane pathway, and scores the governed revision against ten defects frozen before the runner was generalized.”

## Suggested governance narration

“The baseline caught all ten defects. Human review still found a different issue: the capacity record had combined Maya’s activity with an OT simulation. We preserved the passing baseline, logged the defect, fixed the reusable logic, and reran every check. That is the point of Neverlost—validation does not replace review.”

## Closing boundary

End with a visible note: “Synthetic demonstration only. No medical, vocational, benefits, funding, or operational decision is made by this workflow.”
