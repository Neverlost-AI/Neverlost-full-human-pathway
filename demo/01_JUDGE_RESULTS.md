# Neverlost: Judge Results Summary

## The problem

AI can turn scattered records into a confident-looking answer while silently converting hopes into capacity, observations into proof, possibilities into approvals, and generic information into personal guarantees.

Neverlost is designed to make those transformations visible and govern them before the output reaches a human decision-maker.

## What was built

Neverlost: The Full Human Pathway combines three reusable skills:

- governed source review and controlled revision;
- whole-person pathway and cross-system bridge mapping; and
- capacity-aware output that keeps conditions, variability, and recovery cost attached to completed work.

The Build Week prototype packages those skills with seven data contracts, a synthetic workflow runner, deterministic validators, a frozen defect scorer, negative tests, and governed checkpoints.

## What was demonstrated

The first synthetic case established the pipeline. A materially different second case then introduced seven records across client, provider, occupational-therapy, historical-employment, vocational-rehabilitation, benefits-information, and candidate-draft authority.

The second case planted ten unsafe transformations. Before any case-specific correction:

| Measure | Locked baseline result |
| --- | ---: |
| Deliberate defects detected and corrected | 10 / 10 |
| Finding classifications matched | 10 / 10 |
| Critical false positives | 0 |
| Qualitative gates | 12 / 12 |
| Final status | `READY_FOR_USER_REVIEW` |

Human inspection then found a separate measurement-fidelity issue: the capacity record combined client activity with a distinct OT simulation. Neverlost’s governed process logged the issue, preserved the passing baseline, applied one reusable patch, and reran the full suite.

Final result:

| Measure | Final result |
| --- | ---: |
| Packet checks | 24 / 24 |
| Unit tests | 6 / 6 |
| Expected pathway lanes | 6 / 6 |
| Authority-bearing bridges | 5 / 5 |
| Governed patch cycles | 1 |
| Prohibited claims retained | 0 |
| Real identifiers present | 0 |

## Why this is stronger than a polished answer

- The answer key was frozen before the runner was generalized.
- The first distinct-case result was committed before scoring or patching.
- Missed defects and false positives were measurable.
- A human-review finding was preserved rather than hidden.
- The correction changed reusable logic instead of manually rewriting the final case.
- Earlier-case regressions and negative prohibited-claim tests still pass.

## What the result supports

Within the synthetic boundary, Neverlost demonstrates source custody, authority separation, conservative capacity interpretation, cross-system planning, controlled revision, reproducible validation, and human-governed improvement.

It does not establish professional accuracy, real-client readiness, counselor endorsement, eligibility, funding, work capacity, marketplace approval, or operational use.
