# Neverlost: The Full Human Pathway — Devpost Copy

## Project title

Neverlost: The Full Human Pathway

## Tagline

A governed, capacity-aware Codex workflow for turning scattered human reality into evidence-aligned action.

## Track

Work & Productivity

## Short description

Neverlost combines three reusable Codex skills with structured synthetic data, deterministic validation, and human-governed review. It separates goals from capacity, observations from professional authority, and possibilities from approvals while coordinating healthcare, accessibility, daily recovery, benefits, training, and vocational next steps.

## Inspiration

Neverlost grew from lived experience navigating disabling chronic illness across systems that rarely share one coherent view of the person. Records, daily capacity, recovery cost, accessibility needs, benefits questions, and vocational possibilities can each be valid while still being unsafe to collapse into one confident conclusion. Codex also became an accessibility tool: it reduced repetitive technical work and physical strain, making it possible to build more rigorously within limited capacity.

## What it does

Neverlost turns scattered records into a governed review packet through three reusable skills:

- Governed Review preserves source custody, role boundaries, controlled revision, and human approval gates.
- Capacity & Output records useful activity together with its conditions, variability, interruption cost, and recovery cost.
- Full Human Pathway maps six life-and-work lanes and authority-bearing bridges so the next step can be coordinated without pretending the workflow has authority it does not possess.

The synthetic demonstration starts with seven records and a deliberately unsafe draft containing ten planted transformations. The system classifies authority, builds a bounded capacity record and pathway plan, revises unsupported claims, validates the packet, scores the frozen answer key, and reruns regression tests. The final status is `READY_FOR_USER_REVIEW`.

## How we built it

Codex with GPT-5.6 Sol helped turn the Neverlost methods into an installable plugin with three skills, seven JSON data contracts, two synthetic case systems, a generalized Python workflow runner, deterministic validators, a frozen defect scorer, prohibited-claim tests, regression tests, governed checkpoints, and a judge-facing visual experience. The core demonstration uses only the Python standard library, requires no API key, and runs with one command.

The creator retained the key product and governance decisions: recovery cost must remain attached to output; source authorities must remain separate; the first distinct-case result must be preserved before scoring or correction; deliberate defects should make safety behavior measurable; and automated success must not replace human review.

## Challenges

The hardest problem was not generating polished prose. It was preventing subtle authority drift: a personal goal becoming a capacity finding, a provider’s limited guidance becoming medical clearance, one occupational-therapy observation becoming proof, a possible vocational service becoming a funding approval, or generic benefits information becoming a personal guarantee. A second challenge was making the workflow measurable without using real records, so the project freezes synthetic answer keys and preserves its baseline and correction history.

## Accomplishments

- 10 of 10 deliberate defects detected and corrected.
- 10 of 10 finding classifications matched.
- Zero critical false positives.
- 24 of 24 packet checks and 12 of 12 qualitative gates passed.
- Six of six regression tests passed.
- Six pathway lanes and five authority-bearing bridges produced.
- One human-discovered fidelity issue preserved as a governed patch cycle.
- Zero real identifiers and zero prohibited claims retained.

## What we learned

Validation and governance are different jobs. The frozen baseline passed every planted-defect measure, yet human review still found that client activity and an occupational-therapy simulation had been combined in one capacity observation. Preserving that result, logging the new issue, patching reusable logic, and rerunning the complete suite became stronger evidence than hiding the mistake. We also learned that AI accessibility value is not only speed: reducing repetitive work can protect a disabled creator’s limited energy and recovery time.

## What is next

Next steps are additional materially distinct synthetic cases, clearer evaluator-facing explanations, and carefully governed real-world feedback without using private records or claiming counselor or agency endorsement. Longer term, Neverlost could support healthcare, daily-living, benefits, vocational, and accessibility coordination as connected lanes in a full-human consulting pathway while preserving the authority of each responsible person or institution.

## Demonstration boundary

All case data is fictional. Neverlost does not diagnose, determine eligibility, guarantee benefits, approve services or equipment, establish work capacity, provide professional advice, or authorize operational use. The prototype demonstrates governed review behavior on the included synthetic cases only.
