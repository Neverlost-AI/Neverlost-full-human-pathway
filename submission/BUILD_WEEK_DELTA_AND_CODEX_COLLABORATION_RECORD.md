## Creator decisions that controlled the product

Jeff Summerhays / Neverlost supplied and controlled the decisions that define the product:

- the Full Human Pathway as the unifying human-centered framework;
- the requirement that capacity include conditions, variability, accommodations, and recovery cost—not output alone;
- the governed-review model and separation of evidence, user report, synthesis, and third-party authority;
- the Work & Productivity positioning and the decision to demonstrate vocational relevance without claiming counselor endorsement;
- the use of deliberate defects and a distinct second case to prove the workflow rather than relying on a polished example;
- the rule that baseline evidence must be preserved before scoring or correction;
- the one-page blue/white NVLT stage-document system and broader product presentation direction;
- the decision to keep real records, operational use, medical conclusions, funding decisions, and unsupported approval claims outside the prototype; and
- the decision to keep Modern Paradox Studio as a separate project and potential submission.
