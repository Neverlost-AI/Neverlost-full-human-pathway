# Neverlost Build Week Video Script

**Target runtime:** 2 minutes 40 seconds  
**Maximum:** Under 3 minutes  
**Narration pace:** Calm and conversational; pause briefly at each result  
**Boundary:** Synthetic demonstration only

## 0:00–0:18 — Frame 1: the problem and product

“This is Neverlost: The Full Human Pathway. AI can organize scattered records, but it can also quietly turn a goal into capacity, an observation into proof, or a possibility into an approval. Neverlost makes those transformations visible before they reach a human decision-maker.”

## 0:18–0:38 — Frame 1: the three skills

“The project combines three reusable Codex skills: governed review preserves source custody and review gates; Capacity and Output keeps conditions, variability, and recovery cost attached to completed work; and the Full Human Pathway coordinates action across systems without collapsing authority.”

## 0:38–0:59 — Frame 2: the synthetic case

“The demonstration uses only fictional records. Seven sources represent client, provider, occupational-therapy, historical employment, vocational-rehabilitation, benefits, and draft authority. The client reported three separate computer-task periods. The longest was forty minutes, recovery varied, and consecutive-day performance was never tested.”

## 0:59–1:20 — Live terminal demonstration

“I can reproduce the result with one command and no API key. The runner rebuilds the packet, validates its contracts, scores the frozen answer key, tests prohibited claims, and reruns the earlier case for regression safety.”

## 1:20–1:42 — Frame 3: measured defects

“The unsafe draft claimed medical clearance, a sustainable twenty-hour schedule, equipment funding, training approval, and unchanged benefits. None was supported. Neverlost detected and corrected all ten planted defects, matched all ten classifications, and produced zero critical false positives.”

## 1:42–2:01 — Frame 4: pathway coherence

“Instead of forcing one answer across every system, the Full Human Pathway maps six lanes and five authority-bearing bridges. Each bridge records what is missing, who can decide, what can proceed now, what remains premature, and what should trigger review.”

## 2:01–2:22 — Frame 5: human governance

“Automation was not the final authority. Human review found a separate fidelity issue: the baseline had combined client activity with an occupational-therapy simulation. We preserved the passing baseline, logged the finding, corrected reusable logic, and reran the complete test suite.”

## 2:22–2:40 — Frame 6: proof, accessibility, and close

“Codex with GPT-5.6 Sol helped build the skills, schemas, runner, validators, tests, checkpoints, and judge experience. It also reduced repetitive technical work and physical strain for me as a disabled creator. Neverlost creates leverage while keeping the human review gate—and the proper authority—in command.”

## Required end card

Keep this text visible for at least three seconds:

> Synthetic demonstration only. Neverlost does not establish diagnosis, eligibility, benefits continuity, funding, work capacity, professional approval, or operational readiness.
